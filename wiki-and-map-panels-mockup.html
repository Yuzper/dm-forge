Wiki panels, and what stays on the map

Needs attention, recently updated and articles by type move into the wiki as native furniture rather than three more cards. The hub keeps only what the map can answer, plus four new overlays that only make sense over a map.

1 · The three, absorbed into the wiki

Counts land on the filter pills you already have, recency becomes a sort plus a resume strip, and needs attention becomes a filter over the grid. Click the pills, the sort, and the attention chip.

The attention chip only appears when there is something wrong, and its three reasons become sub-filters — so the maintenance list is the grid, at full width, sortable, with the reason on the card.

2 · The hub, with only map-answerable overlays

Three left: locations, threads, pressure. Four new: the party and their trail, layers, travel, ambience. Toggle the rail chips on the right; the party strip and trail are always on.

Locations, threads and pressure sit in the drawer; layers, travel and ambience are controls, which is exactly what a drawer is good at. The party strip is not a panel — it is always visible, like a status bar.
